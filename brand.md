# Soft Rounds Australia Brand Book

## 1. Brand Identity

Brand name:

Soft Rounds Australia

Customer-facing short name:

Soft Rounds

Business category:

Premium healthcare apparel and comfort essentials

Initial product categories:

- Nursing compression socks
- Healthcare scrubs

Future product categories may include:

- Scrub caps
- Badge reels
- Pocket organisers
- Shift accessories
- Healthcare gifts

Primary audience:

- Registered nurses
- Enrolled nurses
- Nursing students
- Aged-care workers
- Disability-support workers
- Allied health professionals
- Healthcare assistants
- Other professionals working long or physically demanding shifts

## 2. Brand Purpose

Soft Rounds exists to make long healthcare shifts feel more comfortable,
practical and professionally put together.

The brand should recognise that healthcare workers spend long hours:

- Standing
- Walking
- Moving between tasks
- Supporting patients
- Working under physical and emotional pressure

Products should be presented as thoughtfully designed work essentials rather
than fashion products alone.

## 3. Brand Promise

Primary brand promise:

Premium comfort made for long shifts.

Supporting brand statements:

- Soft, durable essentials for healthcare professionals.
- Designed for demanding shifts and everyday comfort.
- Professional essentials that work as hard as you do.
- Comfort and confidence throughout your shift.

Do not use every statement at once. Select the statement that best suits the
page or campaign.

## 4. Brand Positioning

Soft Rounds Australia should be positioned as:

- Premium but approachable
- Professional but not clinical
- Modern but not trend-dependent
- Comfortable but still polished
- Friendly but not childish
- Practical but not basic
- Calm but not boring

The brand should not resemble:

- A discount medical-supply store
- A generic marketplace
- A hospital equipment website
- A highly feminine beauty brand
- A loud streetwear label
- A novelty gift shop

## 5. Brand Values

### Comfort

Products should support customers through long and demanding workdays.

### Quality

Materials, construction and product information should communicate reliability
and thoughtful production.

### Practicality

Every design decision should make the product or shopping experience easier to
use.

### Professional Confidence

Customers should feel comfortable and appropriately presented at work.

### Honesty

Product claims must be specific, credible and supportable.

### Respect

The brand should communicate respectfully about healthcare work and the people
performing it.

## 6. Brand Personality

Soft Rounds should feel:

- Warm
- Calm
- Dependable
- Thoughtful
- Modern
- Professional
- Supportive
- Confident
- Refined
- Human

Soft Rounds should not feel:

- Loud
- Aggressive
- Overly playful
- Childish
- Cold
- Clinical
- Pretentious
- Overly luxurious
- Generic
- Sales-driven

## 7. Voice and Tone

The brand voice should be:

- Clear
- Warm
- Concise
- Helpful
- Reassuring
- Credible
- Customer-focused

The brand should speak like a knowledgeable and supportive colleague, not like
a medical authority.

### Preferred language

Use language such as:

- Made for long shifts
- Soft and comfortable
- Designed for everyday wear
- Durable construction
- Easy to move in
- Practical storage
- Comfortable fit
- Thoughtfully designed
- Created for healthcare professionals

### Avoid language such as:

- Best ever
- Number one
- Revolutionary
- Life-changing
- Miracle
- Guaranteed relief
- Cure
- Treatment
- Doctor approved
- Clinically proven
- Medical grade

Restricted or clinical claims must only be used when properly substantiated and
legally permitted.

## 8. Writing Style

Website copy should:

- Lead with the customer benefit
- Use short paragraphs
- Use meaningful headings
- Avoid unnecessary jargon
- Explain materials clearly
- State limitations honestly
- Make sizing and care instructions easy to understand
- Use Australian English

Preferred Australian spelling includes:

- Colour
- Personalised
- Organised
- Centre
- Favourite

Product descriptions should explain:

1. What the product is
2. Who it is designed for
3. Its practical benefits
4. Materials
5. Fit
6. Care instructions
7. Relevant sizing or compression information
8. Delivery and returns information

## 9. Visual Personality

The visual identity should feel:

- Soft
- Spacious
- Calm
- Premium
- Contemporary
- Clean
- Tactile
- Professional
- Welcoming

The visual identity should avoid:

- Bright medical blue as the dominant colour
- Excessive pink
- Neon colours
- Harsh black-and-white contrast throughout
- Heavy gradients
- Large drop shadows
- Excessive rounded shapes
- Medical cross symbols as the main identity
- Hearts, feathers or infinity symbols
- Cartoon nursing imagery
- Decorative script fonts

## 11. Colour Balance

Recommended approximate colour balance:

- 55% Warm Ivory or Soft White
- 25% Deep Charcoal and neutral text
- 12% Muted Slate Blue
- 5% Soft Sage
- 3% Soft Clay

This is a guide, not a strict mathematical requirement.

Do not use all accent colours equally in every section.

## 12. Logo Direction

The final logo system should include:

- Primary horizontal logo
- Compact horizontal logo
- Brand icon
- Favicon
- Dark version
- Light version
- Single-colour version

Preferred logo characteristics:

- Modern
- Minimal
- Softly structured
- Professional
- Recognisable at small sizes
- Easy to embroider or print
- Suitable for website, labels and packaging

Possible icon direction:

- A minimal rounded scrub-pocket form
- A subtle stitch or seam detail
- A compact abstract shape associated with softness and professional apparel

Avoid:

- Medical crosses
- Hearts
- Infinity symbols
- Feathers
- Stethoscopes as the primary logo
- Generic nurse silhouettes
- Complex illustrations
- Very thin lines
- Details that disappear at favicon size

Logo status:

Not finalised.

Do not invent a permanent logo inside the Shopify theme until approved brand
assets are supplied.

## 13. Typography Direction

The typography system should use:

- One primary heading font
- One highly readable body font
- A limited number of font weights

Heading font qualities:

- Modern
- Clean
- Softly geometric or humanist
- Confident
- Readable

Body font qualities:

- Neutral
- Highly readable
- Clear on mobile
- Suitable for product information
- Suitable for long descriptions

Avoid:

- Script fonts
- Condensed fonts for body copy
- Very thin weights
- Highly decorative fonts
- Unlicensed font files

Final font selection status:

Pending review through Shopify’s available font library.

## 14. Iconography

Preferred icon style:

- Outline icons
- Rounded line endings
- Consistent stroke thickness
- Simple geometry
- Minimal detail

Preferred stroke width:

- Approximately 1.5px to 2px depending on icon size

Common icon subjects may include:

- Comfort
- Breathability
- Durability
- Stretch
- Pockets
- Easy care
- Shipping
- Returns
- Secure payment
- Size guide

Do not mix several unrelated icon families.

Icons must not replace important text labels where meaning could become unclear.

## 15. Photography Direction

Photography should feel:

- Natural
- Softly lit
- Premium
- Realistic
- Calm
- Inclusive
- Professionally styled
- Relevant to healthcare work

Recommended photography categories:

### Product Photography

- Front view
- Back view
- Side view
- Close-up fabric detail
- Stitching detail
- Pocket detail
- Waistband detail
- Sock compression or knit detail
- Packaging
- Colour comparison

### Lifestyle Photography

- Healthcare professionals wearing the products
- Natural standing and walking poses
- Break-room or preparation moments
- Professional environments
- Comfortable movement
- Details of products in use

### Avoid

- Unrealistic hospital scenes
- Inaccurate clinical procedures
- Generic stock-photo doctors
- Heavy beauty retouching
- Product colours that do not match reality
- AI-generated product details that differ from the real item
- Images that imply unsupported medical outcomes

## 16. Product Presentation

Products should be shown as premium professional essentials.

Each product should clearly communicate:

- Product purpose
- Fit
- Material
- Key features
- Available sizes
- Available colours
- Care requirements
- Delivery expectations
- Return eligibility

Compression sock product pages should clearly state:

- Compression level when verified
- Size measurements
- Fit guidance
- Materials
- Care instructions
- Relevant usage limitations

Do not imply that compression products diagnose, prevent, treat or cure medical
conditions unless the claim is properly supported and legally permitted.

## 17. Packaging Direction

Packaging should feel:

- Clean
- Compact
- Premium
- Practical
- Easy to recycle where possible
- Suitable for gifting

Preferred packaging colours:

- Warm Ivory
- Soft White
- Deep Charcoal
- Muted Slate Blue
- Small Soft Sage or Soft Clay details

Packaging should avoid:

- Excessive plastic
- Overly glossy finishes
- Crowded information
- Unnecessary decorative elements
- Unsupported sustainability claims

## 18. User Experience Principles

The customer experience should be:

- Fast
- Calm
- Predictable
- Easy to navigate
- Mobile-first
- Accessible
- Transparent

Customers should quickly understand:

- What Soft Rounds sells
- Who the products are for
- Why the products are useful
- How to choose a size
- When the order will arrive
- How returns work
- How to contact the business

## 19. Trust Principles

Trust should be established through:

- Accurate product information
- Clear sizing
- Genuine photography
- Transparent pricing
- Clear delivery information
- Clear returns information
- Accessible contact details
- Genuine customer reviews
- Secure Shopify checkout
- Honest stock information

Do not use:

- Fake countdown timers
- Fake stock counters
- Invented reviews
- Invented customer numbers
- Unsupported awards
- False endorsements
- Misleading before-and-after claims

## 20. Brand Consistency Rules

Before creating a new visual or written asset, confirm that it follows:

- This brand book
- `design.md`
- Approved logo files
- Approved product information
- Approved legal and medical claims

When the documents conflict:

1. Safety and legal accuracy take priority.
2. Approved current brand decisions take priority over older drafts.
3. Website interface behaviour should follow `design.md`.
4. Brand identity and communication should follow `brand.md`.
5. Claude working behaviour should follow `AGENTS.md`.
