# Soft Rounds Australia Storefront Mockup

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the approved storefront structure and visual direction for
Soft Rounds Australia.

It should be read together with:

- `brand.md`
- `design.md`
- `PROJECT_PLAN.md`
- `AGENTS.md`

The website should feel:

- Premium
- Calm
- Soft
- Professional
- Spacious
- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Easy to shop

The website should not feel:

- Clinical
- Crowded
- Overly feminine
- Cheap
- Generic
- Loud
- Over-animated

---

## 2. Global Layout

### Desktop

Maximum page width:

- 1440px

Primary content width:

- Approximately 1280px

Editorial text width:

- Approximately 726px to 760px

Horizontal padding:

- 32px to 48px

Standard section spacing:

- 64px

Large feature section spacing:

- 96px

### Mobile

Horizontal padding:

- 16px

Standard section spacing:

- 48px

Content should stack vertically with clear separation between sections.

Avoid horizontal scrolling.

---

## 3. Global Visual Rules

### Backgrounds

Primary background:

- Warm Ivory
- `#F7F5F0`

Card and elevated surface:

- Soft White
- `#FFFFFF`

Dark text:

- Deep Charcoal
- `#252724`

Primary accent:

- Muted Slate Blue
- `#718696`

Secondary accent:

- Soft Sage
- `#A9B5A3`

Warm accent:

- Soft Clay
- `#D69B88`

### Corners

- Buttons: 8px
- Form fields: 8px
- Product cards: 12px
- Large images and panels: 16px
- Tags and compact pills: fully rounded

### Shadows

Use subtle shadows only.

Product cards should normally use:

- No shadow, or
- Very soft shadow on hover

Prefer borders and spacing over heavy shadows.

## 6. Homepage

### Section 1 — Hero

Purpose:

Immediately explain what Soft Rounds sells and who it is for.

Suggested headline:

> Premium comfort made for long shifts.

Suggested supporting text:

> Soft, durable compression socks and healthcare scrubs designed for demanding
> workdays.

Primary button:

> Shop compression socks

Secondary button:

> Explore scrubs

Desktop layout:

- Large two-column or full-width image composition
- Text positioned clearly without covering important product detail
- Image should show a real healthcare professional or accurate product styling
- Hero should occupy approximately 70% to 85% of the first viewport

Mobile layout:

- Mobile-specific image
- Text placed below or over a clean image area
- Buttons stacked or full-width
- No tiny text
- No excessive hero height

Hero image must not be lazy-loaded.

---

## 7. Product Cards

Each card should contain:

- Primary image
- Optional second image on hover
- Product title
- Price
- Compare-at price where applicable
- Sale label only when accurate
- Sold-out label only when accurate
- Colour information where useful

Product image ratio:

- Consistent across the collection
- Prefer approximately 4:5 for apparel and socks

Desktop behaviour:

- Subtle image change on hover
- Subtle card lift or border change
- Product remains fully usable without hover

Mobile behaviour:

- No hover dependency
- Two cards per row where readable
- Clear price and title
- Large tap area

Avoid:

- Fake urgency
- Fake ratings
- Fake stock counts
- Excessive badges
- Quick-add behaviour that causes variant confusion

---

## 8. Collection Page

Top structure:

1. Collection title
2. Short collection description
3. Product count
4. Sort control
5. Filters
6. Product grid

Description should be concise and should not push products too far down the page.

Desktop grid:

- Three or four columns depending on viewport width

Tablet grid:

- Two or three columns

Mobile grid:

- Two columns where readable

Filters may include:

- Size
- Colour
- Product type
- Price
- Availability

Compression filters should only include verified product attributes.

## 10. Compression Sock Product Information

Each compression sock listing should clearly state verified information such as:

- Compression level
- Size measurements
- Fibre composition
- Length
- Fit
- Care instructions
- Colour or pattern
- Usage guidance supplied by the manufacturer

Avoid stating that the product:

- Treats a medical condition
- Prevents blood clots
- Cures swelling
- Guarantees pain relief
- Is medical grade without evidence

Use careful, factual language.

---

## 11. Scrub Product Information

Each scrub listing should clearly state:

- Garment type
- Fabric composition
- Stretch
- Fit
- Pocket details
- Waistband details
- Size measurements
- Care instructions
- Colour
- Model sizing where applicable

The product page should prioritise:

- Comfort
- Movement
- Practical storage
- Durability
- Easy care

Do not use unverified claims such as stain-proof, antimicrobial or wrinkle-free
unless confirmed by testing or supplier documentation.

## 15. Mobile Experience

Mobile is the primary design priority.

Requirements:

- No horizontal overflow
- Readable 16px body text
- Clear tap targets
- Simple navigation
- Two-column product grids only when readable
- Full-width purchasing buttons
- No hover-dependent controls
- Minimal animation
- Easy cart access
- Easy size-guide access
- Fast image loading

Test at:

- 375px
- 430px

---

## 16. Motion and Interaction

Use subtle motion only.

Allowed:

- Button hover feedback
- Product image crossfade
- Drawer opening and closing
- Small section reveal
- Focus transitions

Avoid:

- Parallax
- Continuous animation
- Auto-moving carousels
- Large zoom effects
- Animation that delays interaction

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

## 17. Homepage Content Readiness

Sections may remain hidden until their content is genuine.

Do not publish incomplete sections containing:

- Placeholder reviews
- Placeholder products
- Generic stock photography
- Unverified claims
- Missing links
- Draft policy information

A smaller complete homepage is preferable to a large unfinished homepage.
